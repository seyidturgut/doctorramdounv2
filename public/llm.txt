# Dr. Abdulalim Ramdoun - Expert Physiotherapy & Rehabilitation Center

## About
Dr. Abdulalim Ramdoun is a leading expert in physiotherapy and rehabilitation, specializing in neurological and orthopedic recovery for international patients in Turkey. With over 10 years of experience, he provides personalized care plans combining advanced medical technology with compassionate support.

## Services
- **Neurological Rehabilitation:** Stroke recovery, spinal cord injury, robotic-assisted therapy.
- **Orthopedic Rehabilitation:** Post-op recovery, sports injuries, joint mobilization.
- **Deep Brain Stimulation (DBS) Support:** Guidance and facilitation for Parkinson's patients.
- **Smart Spinal Stimulation:** Advanced chronic pain management.
- **Manual Therapy:** Spine care, myofascial release.
- **Psychological Support:** Mental health support for rehabilitation patients.
