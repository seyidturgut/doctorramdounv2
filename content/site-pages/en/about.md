## Meet your rehabilitation specialist

With more than a decade of experience in general medicine and rehabilitation, Dr. Abdulalim Ramdoun focuses on early evaluation, personalized care plans, and internationally coordinated treatment journeys.

## Clinical philosophy

- Every patient requires an individualized recovery roadmap
- Effective rehabilitation must consider physical, neurological, and psychological factors together
- Technology should support compassionate decision-making, not replace it

## International patient experience

Dr. Ramdoun has worked with international patients across multiple countries and continues to support complex treatment planning for patients traveling to Turkey.
