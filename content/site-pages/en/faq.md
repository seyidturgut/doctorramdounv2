## Common patient questions

### Travel and arrival

Patients usually ask about airport pickup, accommodation support, timing of the first consultation, and how quickly treatment can start.

### Insurance and communication

Many international patients also ask about insurance review, medical documentation, and language support during treatment planning.

### Follow-up after treatment

Digital follow-up remains important for long-term rehabilitation, especially for patients returning home after care in Turkey.
