## Core rehabilitation services

- **Neurological rehabilitation** for stroke recovery, spinal cord conditions, and motor function improvement
- **Orthopedic rehabilitation** for post-operative care, joint recovery, and return-to-mobility programs
- **Manual therapy** for spine care, pain relief, and movement restoration
- **Deep Brain Stimulation support** for patients exploring advanced Parkinson's-related care
- **Smart spinal stimulation support** for complex pain-management cases
- **Psychological support** for confidence, adaptation, and treatment continuity

## Service model

Each plan is built around the patient's diagnosis, current mobility level, treatment goals, and travel realities.
